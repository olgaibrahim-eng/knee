Reminders from your plan

Use a comfortable range and reduce the dose if pain rises. Aim to return to your usual pain level by the following morning.

On stairs, use a handrail and one step at a time when sore. Do not force deep squats or repeated stair exercises.

Move forward when exercises feel manageable, stairs are improving and symptoms are not worse the next day. You can repeat a week if needed. This tracker does not replace an assessment by a physiotherapist.

Walking and low impact activity

Log walking, cycling, Pilates, yoga or rest days. Adjust or avoid movements that aggravate your knee.
